About Me

Rachel L. Harari
Architect
Registered in: NJ, NY

Rachel is a passionate architect dedicated to designing unique, functional spaces that reflect each client’s vision. With a focus on creativity, practicality, and collaboration, she combines aesthetic elegance with real-world solutions to deliver exceptional architecture. Rachel works closely with all clients to ensure every detail is thoughtfully considered and executed. 

With a deep interest in travel, design inspiration is drawn from diverse cultures and environments, bringing a global perspective to every project. Artistic by nature, Rachel enjoys exploring different mediums, from sketching to digital design, to bring fresh, innovative ideas to life.
